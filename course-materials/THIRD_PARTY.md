# Third-Party Materials and Tools

## Bundled

- `paper/neurips_2026.sty`: unmodified official NeurIPS 2026 template material. It is
  included for inspectable paper source and is not claimed as original content.

## Referenced, not bundled

- EvalPlus (NeurIPS 2023), SWE-agent (NeurIPS 2024), CLEVER (NeurIPS 2025),
  and ReLoc (NeurIPS 2025) are cited research works; their paper text, data, and
  repositories are excluded from generated release packages. Local reference
  PDFs, when present in a working directory, are not teaching artifacts.
- Stanford CS329A, Weak Verifiers, RLEF, CodeMonkeys, and the METR long-task
  study are linked as optional instructor context in
  `materials/05_supplementary_readings.md`; none of their content or code is
  bundled.
- Python, pytest, Jupyter/nbconvert, Pandoc, and TeX Live are separately
  installed tools governed by their own licenses.

## Originality boundary

Prior ASE/ISE teaching experience motivates the pedagogy but previously
published course artifacts are not bundled. The submitted labs, repositories,
templates, slides, notebook, pilot instruments, and build scripts were created
for this Education Track package. See `MANIFEST.md`.
