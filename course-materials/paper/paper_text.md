# Teaching Verification-Centered Software Engineering with Coding Agents

The PDF in this package is the two-page Education Track summary. Its required sections are: The Concept; Leveling and Prerequisite Knowledge; Learning Objectives and Outcomes; Linked Papers; Teaching Materials Summary.

Core concept: the learner—not the agent—is the primary object of improvement.
Learners convert agent-produced candidates into evidence-backed changes by
validating intent, constraining the ACI, separating correctness evidence from
revision guidance and model opinion, calibrating a property-scoped claim, and making a distinct
architecture/security approval decision.
